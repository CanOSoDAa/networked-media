SO... WHY SODA?

ABOUT
THE FIZZ.

Soda is more than a drink. It's color, packaging, advertising, nostalgia and a tiny explosion every time you open the can.

Soda Pops! is a small web collection inspired by the visual culture surrounding soft drinks: loud colors, bold typography, ridiculous advertisements, shiny cans and that universally recognizable pssshhh sound.

This site explores those personalities through a playful digital soda bar. Each drink gets its own visual world, because no two pops fizz exactly the same.
